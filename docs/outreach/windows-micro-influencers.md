## Outreach notes

- Re-check the audience size, current publishing cadence, email address, and product fit immediately before contacting anyone.
- Personalise each message around the creator’s relevant Windows coverage; do not send an undifferentiated bulk email.
- Treat the addresses as business-contact data only. Follow applicable anti-spam and privacy rules, include a clear identity and purpose, and honour opt-out requests.
- NXT Windows is the smallest and potentially most accessible lead. WindowsChimp, Tech Based, and Tweak Library sit near the upper end of the working micro-influencer definition.
